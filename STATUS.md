## Deviations from the brief (found while modelling — fold into the brief at the end)
- **Body size and seat length.** Bolt-variant body seats lengthen automatically so the bolt bosses never stick out past a seat end (61 / 68 mm at the defaults; `seat_len` becomes a minimum). An oblique end would leave knife edges at the seat mouths.
- **Body height.** The body is built as a lower block (seat 1) + 45° transition + upper block (seat 2); the transition height is computed from the overhang. The body is 42.8 mm tall instead of about 38, so the bolts are M6 × 75 or M6 × 80 (72 mm minimum) instead of about M6 × 70.
- **Corner radius.** Seat-end corners have a 0.5–1.2 mm radius instead of 4 mm, which would thin the seat wall to 0.25 mm. In the zip-tie variant the block corner radius is also limited by the tie slots: 4 / 1.75 mm at the defaults; R4 in the upper block would leave about 0.9 mm beside the pipe-2 slots. Recorded as a design-rule deviation.
- **Tie-channel bend radius.** `tie_bend_r` is a minimum; it grows automatically for wide ties (instead of a guard refusing them).
- **Transition height.** Computed from the exact (rounded) outline of the lower block — the sharp-corner estimate gave 45.7° at `seat_len2` = 80.
- **Gauge notches.** Now exact through the plate thickness; the first version was 1.4–1.8 mm oversize because one hull spanned both face flares.
- **Caps and bolts (v1.4, after requester review).** Four through-bolts instead of two (two diagonal bolts put every clamping force on one line, so the clamp could rock about it); layout "corners" or "pairs" by angle, whichever is more compact. Bosses keep the full bolt wall at the top face. The v1.0–1.3 caps (a hump with two 6 mm wings and 4 mm over the V apex) were too weak: the wings flexed (wobble), the wing roots would delaminate, and the section over the apex reached about 50 MPa at 300 N per bolt. Each cap is now one solid convex block with counterbores (cap 1) or captive-nut hex wells (cap 2), 8 mm floors, and 10 mm over the apex (about 8 MPa). Washer and nut sizes now follow the bolt size list (ISO 7089 / 4032); `washer_d`, `washer_t`, `nut_h`, `flange_t` removed; `bolt_tool_d`, `floor_t` added.
- **`bolt_clear` meaning.** It is the total extra on the hole diameter, including the vertical-hole compensation (default 0.6), so the coupon letter maps directly to it.
- **Size label.** Moved from the body to the gauge (debossed diameters); no clean text area exists on the body at every angle.
- **Zip-tie body size.** 48 × 54 × 62 mm; its tie channels are U-shaped with 45° gabled roofs and 6 mm bends.
- **Context scene.** Uses `include` with a `build_part` switch instead of `use`, so the Customizer overrides reach the scene.

## For the skill maintainers (found on this item)
- **Customizer comments:** OpenSCAD builds a list or slider only when the comment after the value is nothing but the `[spec]`; any text after it gives a plain text box. Put the description on the line above (it becomes the label). The skill's `scad/test_coupon.scad` has the same problem (fixed in this item's coupon copy). `tools/fix_customizer_comments.py` converts a file.
- **Requester preference:** choices as lists (labelled `[value:Label, …]`), numbers as sliders where a range makes sense.
- **`scripts/sweep.py` bug:** any spec containing `:` was read as a range, so labelled lists (`[body:Body, …]`) were mis-read or skipped, and numeric lists were quoted. Patched in this item's `tools/sweep.py` (range = numbers only; quote only string parameters) — please merge into the skill.

- **Multi-part plates:** `scripts/export.sh` and `scripts/sweep.py` assumed one body per STL. Patched in this item's copies: a model may announce its limits with `echo("CHECK expect_bodies=N max_size=M")`, and both scripts pass them to `check_stl.py`. Please merge into the skill.
- **Requester preference:** the part list always offers "all printable parts" as one option.

- **Lesson (for references/lessons.md):** check the section over a clamp's V apex, not only the flange root; never put bolt load into a thin wing that joins a taller block with an inside corner — layers peel there; never clamp with two diagonal bolts — use two per side so no line of bolts exists to rock about; section-check the top face of every well where two chamfers meet.
